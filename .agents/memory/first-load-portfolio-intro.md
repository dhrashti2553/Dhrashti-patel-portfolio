---
name: First-load portfolio intro
description: Keep the portfolio's branded intro from obscuring the main page during an initial visit or preview capture.
---

Keep the intro animation short enough that the hero content appears immediately after the brand moment.

**Why:** Preview captures repeatedly landed on the intro overlay before the portfolio content, which made the app look unfinished even though the page rendered correctly.

**How to apply:** When changing the intro or first-load motion, prioritize a quick handoff to the page and continue honoring reduced-motion preferences.
